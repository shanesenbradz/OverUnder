Privacy policy

Brad's Underover, Android app and web app. Last updated 18 November 2026 (version 1.0).

Brad's Underover is an emulator that runs games you load yourself. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not collect, send or sell any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • ROM files you add, in-game saves, save states, cheats, screenshots and settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Exports, screenshots and video clips you choose to save are written to Downloads/Underover on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app (or clearing site data in the browser) deletes everything it stored, apart from files you exported to Downloads.

Network access

The app doesn't send your games, saves or anything else anywhere. The only connection it makes is the update check: the Android app and the web version can look at a small version file on GitHub to see if a new version is out, and download it if you say yes. The web version is downloaded once from GitHub Pages and then runs offline. GitHub may keep standard server logs for those downloads under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you switch the DS microphone to your phone's mic, Android asks for microphone permission. The sound goes straight into the game while you play and is never recorded or stored.

Permissions

  • Vibration, so on-screen buttons can give haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings, Look, Vibration.
  • Internet, only for the update check and the update download.
  • Microphone, asked for only if you switch the DS microphone to your phone's mic.
  • Install apps, so the app can hand a downloaded update to Android's installer. Android asks you first.
  • Files are opened through the Android file picker, so the app only sees the files you pick.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
